Gait

Walk naturally with the phone in hand, held up or swinging. Steps are detected from the vertical acceleration, and each step is compared with a template for its foot.

Everything is computed in the gravity frame so it doesn't depend on how the phone is held: vertical acceleration along gravity, horizontal acceleration across it, gyro magnitude and screen angle. Each step is resampled to 64 points and assigned to a foot by the direction of the body's slow side-to-side sway at that moment, which alternates with the stepping foot regardless of how the phone is held. Step consistency is the correlation of each step with its foot template; left/right difference is one minus the correlation between the two templates. The lever arm is the distance from the sensor to the point the phone pivots about, fitted from 2 to 6 Hz rotation against acceleration over the last 4 s. Save CSV writes every step window with its features.
