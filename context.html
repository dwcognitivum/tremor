Ground truth

Detection is rule based, from six indicators: low-frequency movement, screen angle from gravity, slow sway of the screen angle, step rhythm, the ratio of rotation to linear vibration in the 4 to 12 Hz band, and gyro power in that band. Thresholds are constants at the top of the script and are first guesses; the logged CSV (four rows a second, indicators plus detected and labelled context) is what tunes them or trains a classifier. Seated and standing are not separated: a first labelled run showed the same sway for both. Lying on your back is read from a flat screen facing downward while in hand; lying on your side is not detectable this way and will read as seated.
