Gesture coupling

Hold the phone as you normally do. Tap Start, then swipe left and right or scroll up and down on the dark pad with your thumb, one stroke at a time. Repeat each direction ten or more times.

Each stroke is recorded with the gyro and accelerometer from 150 ms before touch-down to 300 ms after lift-off, on a shared 10 ms grid. Rotation lag is the delay between finger speed and rotation rate from cross-correlation, reported in whole sensor samples (about 17 ms at 59 Hz) since that is the real resolution; negative means the phone starts turning before the thumb moves. Strokes shorter than 200 ms are rejected so each has at least 12 samples. Coupled rotation is the rotation about the screen axis perpendicular to the stroke (the axis a hand uses to bring the far side of the screen closer), signed relative to the stroke direction, per 100 px of travel. Twist is rotation about the axis through the screen. Consistency is the correlation of this stroke's rotation profile with the mean profile for the same direction. Distances are in CSS pixels; on this phone 100 px is roughly 1.5 to 2 cm.
