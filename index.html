Phone sensor experiments

Each page needs motion access. Safari asks on the first tap of Start.

Micro tremor Live 4 to 12 Hz band from the gyro or accelerometer, with spectrum, movement gate, gait subtraction and impulse rejection. Records both sensors to CSV. Best when sitting with the arm unsupported. Gyro, 4 to 12 Hz, 2 s window to see the oscillation. Tap recoil The phone's response to each tap, aligned at touch-down: peak, ring frequency, settle time and tap-to-tap consistency. Records every tap window to CSV. One clean tap at a time; wait for the pad to return to dark blue between taps. Context detector Says what the phone is doing from the motion sensors: on a surface, in hand seated or standing, held flat, hand moving, walking held up or swinging, vehicle. Log ground truth with one tap. Rules with first-guess thresholds. The CSV it saves is for tuning them. Gait Step detection in the gravity frame, per-foot step templates, cadence, timing spread, step consistency, left/right difference, impact peak and lever arm. Records every step window to CSV. Walk a straight corridor at a natural pace, phone held up or swinging.

Keep index.html, tremor.html, taprecoil.html, context.html and gait.html in the same folder on the host. Files saved from either page go to the phone's downloads or share sheet.
