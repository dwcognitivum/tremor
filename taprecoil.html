Tap recoil

Hold the phone the way you normally use it, tap Start, then tap the dark pad with your thumb, as you would a button. One tap at a time with a clear gap between them; the pad goes grey while it is busy. Twenty or so taps builds a template.

Tap Start first

Each tap captures 100 ms before and 400 ms after touch-down from both sensors, aligned at the touch, with the pre-tap level subtracted. Features per tap: peak accelerometer and gyro response, dominant ring frequency of the gyro, time for the response to settle below 20% of its peak, dwell time and contact size from the touch itself, and correlation of the tap with the running mean. Browsers deliver motion at about 60 samples a second, which is coarse for a response this fast; a native app at 100 to 200 Hz would resolve it much better. Save CSV writes every tap's aligned window in long form.
